Everyone with access works on the same database at the same time.  The people here right now show beside the database's name, and each person's selected cell has an outline in their color.  When someone changes a cell you can see, it flashes once in their color.

## When records move

Riosoma never moves rows under you while you work.  When others add records that belong in your view, or change values your view is sorted or filtered by, a button appears at the top of the grid, such as "{{ui:database.prompt.changed}}".  Press it when you're ready, and the view catches up while keeping your place.

Your own new records stay where you added them until the view refreshes.
