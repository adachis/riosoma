## Changing language

Public releases v0.95 and v1 use American English for the interface, help, narration, and demo media.  Language settings and pickers stay hidden when only English is enabled.  Browser languages, language links, and older preferences for other languages fall back to English.

Self-hosted builds can enable additional languages at build time.  When your build offers more than one language, Riosoma follows your browser's closest enabled language.  To choose one yourself, open {{ui:shell.nav.settings}}, then {{ui:settings.nav.language}}.  Your choice follows you to every device and also sets the language of the emails Riosoma sends you.

Dates, times, and numbers follow the language you choose.

Rio can answer in the language you write, even while its controls stay in English.
