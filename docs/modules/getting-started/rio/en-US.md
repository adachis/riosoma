Rio is a small rhizome who appears when you open a conversation.  Rio's face shows what's happening: thinking while something works, happy when it finishes, and a little startled when something goes wrong.

Leave Rio alone for a while and Rio takes a nap.  Rio wakes up when the conversation picks up again.

If you'd rather not see movement, turn on reduced motion in your computer's accessibility settings.  Rio then stays still and only changes expression.

{{ui:rio.ai}} sits in the top bar on every screen, beside your personal menu.  It opens the conversation, and pressing it again closes it.

In an open conversation, Rio sits at the lower corner, just above the message box, large enough to read.  Rio never covers the message box or its buttons.

In a narrow conversation panel, {{ui:rio.moreActions}} holds {{ui:rio.new}} and {{ui:rio.routines}}.  The conversation selector keeps enough room to choose past conversations.
