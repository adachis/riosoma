Some help topics have a {{ui:demos.showMe}} button under their title.  Choose it, and Riosoma walks you through the steps on your own screen.  It points at each control with an outline, says what it's for, and opens menus or types examples the way you would.

## Nothing changes

A demo works on a copy of what you see.  Whatever it types or chooses shows up as it would, but nothing is saved or sent to anyone, and it isn't added to your undo history.  When the demo ends, everything is back exactly the way it was.

## Following along

A card beside each control shows what the demo is saying, so it works with the sound off, which is how it starts.  Use its buttons to go at your own pace:

- {{ui:demos.pause}} holds the demo on the current step, and {{ui:demos.play}} carries on.
- {{ui:demos.back}} and {{ui:demos.next}} move one step at a time.
- {{ui:demos.narration}} turns spoken narration on or off.  It starts off, and Riosoma remembers your choice.
- {{ui:demos.stop}}, or {{keys:demo.stop}}, ends the demo at once.

Narration follows the interface language, which is American English in public releases, and uses an English recording or your browser's voice.  The demo's controls work from the keyboard and with a screen reader.  If you turn on reduced motion, the demo moves from step to step without animating.

## When a demo stops early

Some demos start on a particular screen, such as a table, and say so if you're somewhere else.  A demo also stops, and says why, when a step would reach something you don't have access to or that's above your level.  See {{doc:levels}} to change your level.
