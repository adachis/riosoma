Riosoma keeps your organization's data in databases you can shape yourself: tables, views, forms, and dashboards, all on a server you run.

## Finding your way around

The bar across the top shows where you are.  The sidebar on the left lists the places you can go, and you can drag its edge to make it wider or narrower.  {{ui:shell.nav.settings}} holds your personal choices, such as your theme and language.

Every page has its own address.  Copy it from the address bar and anyone with access lands in the same place.

To jump straight to a database, table, or view, press {{keys:palette.open}} and type part of its name.  {{doc:palette}} tells you more.

## Getting help

Help opens beside what you're doing, so you can read and work at the same time.  Rio, the little rhizome behind the AI button, is there when you want a hand.

## Contextual help and the documentation window

Open {{ui:shell.help}} from your personal menu, press {{keys:help.open}}, or choose Help in the command palette.  Help opens the topic for your screen and the section for the control or configuration surface you are using.  {{ui:docs.also}} lists other topics that describe the same screen.  A question-mark button in a configuration header opens its section directly.

{{ui:docs.allTopics}} returns to the full topic list.  {{ui:docs.search}} searches titles, summaries, and the text of every topic.  Use the table of contents to open a section.  The address keeps the selected topic and section, so reloading returns to them.

Choose {{ui:docs.openWindow}} to keep documentation beside your work in a separate window inside Riosoma.  Drag its title bar to move it, and drag the lower-right corner to resize it.  Focus the move or resize icon and use the arrow keys to do the same with a keyboard.  The window remembers its size and position, stays within the browser, and becomes a full-screen sheet on a phone.  {{ui:docs.back}} and {{ui:docs.forward}} revisit topics and sections.  {{ui:docs.dock}} returns it to the help panel, and {{ui:docs.download}} keeps a PDF.  Show-me demos and interface labels work in either place.
