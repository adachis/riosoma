Now and then Riosoma marks a milestone.  Rio cheers, and a little confetti in your theme's colors bursts from whatever you just did.  It lasts about a second, never covers the field you're typing in, and never gets in the way of a click.

## What gets celebrated

- Your first table.
- A table reaching 100 records, and then 1,000.
- The first answer to a form you shared publicly.
- An import that finishes without skipping a row.
- A synced table's first sync that works.
- An automation's first run that works.
- Every card on a board reaching its last stack, once the board has at least five.
- Every record in a view checked in a checkbox field, once the view has at least ten.
- The first booking on a booking page.

Each first happens once.  Apart from the biggest moments, you get at most one celebration every ten minutes, however much you get done.

## Turning them down

Open {{ui:shell.nav.settings}}, then {{ui:settings.nav.celebrations}}.  Under {{ui:settings.celebrations.level}}, choose {{ui:settings.celebrations.on}}, {{ui:settings.celebrations.quieter}} for the small ones only, or {{ui:settings.celebrations.off}}.

If your device is set to reduce motion, nothing moves.  A short note from Rio appears in the corner instead.

## Sounds

Sounds start off.  Under {{ui:settings.celebrations.sounds}}, choose {{ui:settings.sounds.quiet}} or {{ui:settings.sounds.normal}} to hear a soft tick when you check a box, a chime or a few rising notes when something's celebrated, and a low tone when something goes wrong.  The buttons below play each one, so you can hear them before choosing.

Riosoma never plays a sound while its tab is out of sight, or for something someone else did.  Your choices follow you to every device where you sign in.
